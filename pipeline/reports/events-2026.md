**The three events most likely to have changed channels:**

1. **Jul 13 pyroclastic density current:** a lava collapse sent a current down the slope at 6:31 to 6:46 a.m., during the peak of the eruption.
2. **Jul 11 to 18 eruption peak:** lava filled the Basud (3.8 km), Bonga (3.2 km) and Mi-isi gullies, and Mi-isi was still lengthening, from 2.1 km on Jul 13 to a final 2.7 km.
3. **Jul 16 lahar in Daraga (unconfirmed):** the only flow in a lowland channel that I could source, from one outlet quoting a resident's photo.

The Oct 30 eruption isn't on this list. Reports so far describe ash only, with no lava flow, current or lahar on any named channel.

**What I couldn't find:**

- **PHIVOLCS bulletins.** Its sites refused every connection from here, so all PHIVOLCS facts come through news reports. This is the biggest gap.
- **Anything from Aug 18 to Oct 19 at Mayon.** I found no sourced event in these two months. That may reflect thin coverage, not a quiet volcano.
- **Quarry actions.** I found none that I could open for the record period, so there are no quarry events. See the leads below.
- **Rainfall in Albay.** I found no rainfall total for Albay and no heavy-rain event. PAGASA's storm reports list only gusts for Legazpi.
- **Rosal's PAGASA report.** It returned a 404, and so did Neneng's, so Albay's gale warning rests on one outlet.
- **The gully of the Jul 13 current.** No page I opened names it.

**The July event was Jul 13, not Jul 14.** GMA, the Tribune and DZRH all date the current to Monday, Jul 13. Asia News Network published on Jul 14 but says Monday. Jul 14 had Strombolian activity, but not this current. Both dates fall in the same pair, so no map marker moves.

**Storm dates** are when PAGASA's signals were up, except Luis, which uses its dates as a cyclone, and Rosal, which uses the dates reported. The Albay readings come from PAGASA's signal maps, which I read by eye. The Maymay and Francisco maps stop short of Albay, so for those two I can only say the signals ended well to the north.

**No Albay signal in the rest of the record.** PAGASA's reports for Gardo, Josie, Obet, Pilandok and Queenie say no signal was raised anywhere. Henry's signal was up for six hours in three provinces, and I didn't check its map. Kiyapo's signals stopped north of Bicol on its map. Neneng has no PAGASA report I could open, and Wikipedia describes its impacts only in Taiwan and China. These storms aren't listed as events.

## Does anything explain the Oct 8 → Oct 20 signal?

**No. I found no event that explains it, and that doesn't rule one out.**

- **Storms:** a cyclone looks unlikely. Queenie left the Philippine area on Sep 27 and Rosal entered on the morning of Oct 20. PAGASA names cyclones in alphabetical order, so no named cyclone came between them. Rosal arrived at about the end of the pair, and its only reported effects on Albay are from Oct 20 onward.
- **Non-cyclone rain:** still open. I found no rain record for those dates. A Philippine Information Agency article appeared in search results saying Bicol had below-normal rain in October, but the page refused to open, so it isn't used as a source. It's worth checking by hand.
- **Volcano:** still open. Without PHIVOLCS bulletins, I can't say what happened at the summit from Oct 8 to 20. The nearest reported readings are from Oct 28 to 30, just before the eruption. The Aug 17 report said tilt stations showed swelling in the north and east, so the volcano wasn't fully at rest, but no source links that to this signal.

## Conflicts between sources

- **Mi-isi length on Jul 13:** PHIVOLCS figures in Philstar and GMA say 2.1 km. A DSWD report quoted by The Watchers says 1.6 km, and also gives Bonga as 3 km and Basud as 4 km. I used the PHIVOLCS figures.
- **Oct 30 ash direction:** PHIVOLCS says west-southwest. Tokyo's ash centre, later that evening, says south.
- **Sulfur dioxide record date:** Vulkane.net says Jun 11. Philstar says Jul 11, which fits the peak, so I used Jul 11.
- **Luis and Maymay:** Wikipedia treats them as one storm (Kujira). PAGASA issued a separate report for each, and I followed PAGASA.
